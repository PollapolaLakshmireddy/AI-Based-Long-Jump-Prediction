Video Submission Instructions

To achieve the highest landmark tracking confidence from our MediaPipe computer vision model, please adhere to these standardized camera placement and recording rules.

📐 1. Camera Angle & Placement

  • Mount camera strictly perpendicular (90° side profile) to the runway.
  • Align camera with the takeoff board line.
  • Set tripod at hip height (~1.1 meters off ground).
  • Distance from runway: 4 to 6 meters.

🎥 2. Framing & Video Capture

  • Capture the final 5m of approach, takeoff board, flight, and landing sandpit.
  • Record in landscape mode (horizontal framing).
  • Minimum 30 FPS (60 FPS recommended for crisp takeoff frame).
  • Resolution: 720p or 1080p standard HD.

🏃 3. Athlete Preparation

  • Wear form-fitting athletic wear with contrasting colors.
  • Ensure clear line-of-sight without bystanders walking through.
  • Keep runway well-lit (natural outdoor daylight or indoor floodlights).
  • Max video duration: 15 seconds (trim excess footage).
